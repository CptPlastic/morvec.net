The short version

Toolbox was already in the back seat

I've always been that person. Toolbox in the back seat. Plan in my head.

My Bronco came home looking like every other one on the lot. No personality in it. So I bought a cutting machine and made the overlays myself.

Friends wanted a set. Then strangers did. The bench hasn't been quiet since. If a piece doesn't line up clean, it doesn't ship.

I like one line of sass you can read from a car length back. I like the look of an old Army supply label. Buyers are neighbors. That's the whole policy.
