I'm the one in the wide-brim hat. Silver curls, olive scarf, a camo jacket that's already done some miles.

I keep a west-facing window and I still watch the light change. Between that and the workbench, that's most of a day.

I cut overlays, stickers, and decals for women who drive with the doors off. Military, first responder, IT. If you want a thing that fits, you're my kind of neighbor.

The short version

Toolbox was already in the back seat

I've always been the one with a toolbox in the back seat. Somewhere along the way I got a cutting machine, and that was the end of my free time.

I started making overlays for my own Bronco because nothing out there had any personality. Friends wanted a set. Then strangers did. Now the bench is covered in vinyl scraps, and I wouldn't have it any other way.

I used to be an engineer, technically. Quiet work. Getting a system to line up. Same hands, different material. If a piece doesn't fit clean, it doesn't leave here.

I drink my coffee black, usually from a thermos. I like a kitchen that smells like something I made.
