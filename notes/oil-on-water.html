Oil on water

Something new is coming to the bench. A laser, a big one. It rolls, and when I need the floor it lowers itself down like it's trying not to be in the way, then climbs back up when I don't.

It'll take metal. I watched it put color into stainless and the burn ran like oil on water — blues and golds I didn't know steel could hold.

I've been standing in the doorway of the shop, coffee gone cold, trying to decide which wall it should face. Nell inspected the empty corner and found it lacking in seats.
