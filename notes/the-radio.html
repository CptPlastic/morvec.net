The radio in the kitchen

I left the radio on again. Didn't notice until I came in from the bench and the room was talking to itself. Some old song I couldn't place.

I stood there with a mug and let it finish. The coffee had gone lukewarm, which is how I knew I'd been gone longer than I thought.
